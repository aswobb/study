## 本课重点

这一课主要练习一般将来时。一般将来时表示将来会发生的动作或状态。

```text
He will go to the market.
This cat will eat this fish.
Tom will stay in this house.
Nobody will join this team.
```

一般将来时最常见的结构是：

```text
主语 + will + 动词原形
```

## will 的基本用法

`will` 后面直接接动词原形，不需要变形。

```text
I will go.
He will come.
They will stay.
```

注意：

- `will` 后面用动词原形：`go / come / eat / attend`
- 不要写成 `will goes`、`will comes`
- 不管主语是 `I / he / she / they`，都用 `will`

```text
I will go to school tomorrow.
She will attend the party tonight.
They will play basketball later.
```

## 否定句

```text
主语 + will not + 动词原形
主语 + won't + 动词原形
```

例如：

```text
I will not go.
I won't go.
He won't stay here.
Nobody will join this team.
```

## 一般疑问句

把 `will` 放到句首，就变成一般疑问句。

```text
Will he go to the market?
Will you attend our wedding?
Will Tom stay in this house?
```

回答方式：

```text
Yes, he will.
No, he won't.
```

## can 和 will 的区别

这节你复习里有一句：

```text
Can you come to my house tomorrow?
```

这里 `can` 表示“能不能、可不可以”，是请求或能力。

而 `will` 更单纯表示将来。

```text
I will come tomorrow.
```

所以：

```text
Can you come tomorrow? 你明天能来吗？
Will you come tomorrow? 你明天会来吗？
```

## come 和 go 的区别

如果目标地点是“说话人这边”，通常用 `come`。

```text
come to my house
come to my home
```

如果是去别的地方，通常用 `go`。

```text
go to school
go to the market
go to the park
```

## be going to

除了 `will`，以后你也会遇到：

```text
be going to + 动词原形
```

比如：

```text
I am going to study tonight.
He is going to play basketball tomorrow.
```

现在阶段先把 `will + 动词原形` 练熟就够了。

## 今日练习

把下面的中文翻译成英文，一律先用 `will` 来写。

```text
1. 我明天会去学校。
I will go to school tomorrow.
2. 她今晚会来看我。
She will come to see me tonight.
3. 他们之后会打篮球。
They will play basketball later.
4. Tom会参加这个聚会吗？
Will Tom attend this party?
5. 这只狗会吃这块肉。
This dog will eat this meat.
6. 我不会留在家里。
I will not stay in my house.
7. 没人会买这本书。
No one will buy this book.
8. 你会加入我们的队伍吗？
Will you join our team?
9. 她会来这个房子吗？
Will she come to this house?
10. 我们明天会去市场。
We will go to the market tomorrow.
```

## 复习检查

把下面的中文翻译成英文：

```text
1. 你明天能来我家吗
Can you go to my home tomorrow?
2. 这只猫将会吃掉这条鱼
This cat will eat this fish.
3. 你会去参加我们的婚礼吗.
Will you attend our wedding?
4. Tom将会留在这栋房里子
Tom will stay in this house.
5. 没人会加入这个队伍
Nobody will join this team.
```

提示：

- 第 1 句如果按中文“来我家”来表达，更自然是 `Can you come to my house tomorrow?`
- `will` 后面一定要接动词原形，比如 `will eat`、`will stay`、`will join`
- `won't` 是 `will not` 的缩写
